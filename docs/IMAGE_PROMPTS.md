# Aurora Confinement Product Illustration Prompts

These are the final built-in ImageGen prompt specifications used for the initial website concept illustrations. The supplied DANTE screenshot was used only as an illustration-style reference. `logonotext.png` was used only as a secondary palette reference.

## Tabletop exhibition model

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: dark website product illustration for Aurora Confinement
Primary request: Create a clean CAD-style technical concept sketch of a tabletop stellarator exhibition model.
Input images: Image 1 is the required illustration-style reference only; match its restrained monochrome CAD linework, flat grey/white surfaces, black outlines, simplified engineering presentation, and translucent purple plasma accent, but do not copy its machine geometry. Image 2 is the Aurora Confinement logo and is a secondary palette reference only; do not reproduce the logo.
Scene/backdrop: pure near-black background with only a few extremely subtle dim cyan star-like points.
Subject: an original compact tabletop exhibit with a clearly non-axisymmetric twisted stellarator coil arrangement around a translucent purple toroidal plasma volume, mounted on a simple low circular display base. It should look like a plausible educational CAD assembly, not a working reactor.
Style/medium: orthographic/isometric CAD screenshot aesthetic, technical line drawing, thin black contour lines, white and light-grey components, sparse flat shading, translucent purple plasma volume with visible construction grid lines. Restrained, diagrammatic, precise.
Composition/framing: centered three-quarter view, complete device visible, generous black margin, square-ish product-card composition.
Constraints: no text, no labels, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no photorealism, no glossy marketing render, no neon glow, no cinematic lighting, no complex internal construction, no patent-sensitive details, not a tokamak, distinctly stellarator-like twisting, original geometry rather than a copy of the reference.
```

## Research-scale platform

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: dark website product illustration for Aurora Confinement
Primary request: Create a clean CAD-style technical concept sketch of a research-scale modular stellarator platform for a university or fusion R&D laboratory.
Input images: Image 1 is the required illustration-style reference only; match its restrained monochrome CAD linework, flat grey/white surfaces, black outlines, simplified engineering presentation, and sparse purple accents, but do not copy its machine geometry. Image 2 is the Aurora Confinement logo and is a secondary palette reference only; do not reproduce the logo.
Scene/backdrop: pure near-black background with only a few extremely subtle dim cyan star-like points.
Subject: an original compact research stellarator assembly on a simple modular support frame. A continuous opaque light-grey sculpted vacuum-vessel shell fully encloses the plasma, so no plasma is directly visible. Distinctive non-axisymmetric external magnetic coils wrap around the enclosed vessel. Include several simplified diagnostic ports and interface modules, plus a restrained equipment cabinet or support module, but keep the assembly clean and understandable.
Style/medium: orthographic/isometric CAD screenshot aesthetic, technical line drawing, thin black contour lines, white and light-grey components, sparse flat shading, selective muted violet accents only on small interface details. Restrained, diagrammatic, precise.
Composition/framing: centered three-quarter view, complete platform visible, generous black margin, landscape-friendly website technology-section composition.
Constraints: plasma must be completely hidden inside the opaque shell; absolutely no glowing plasma tube and no transparent vessel; no text; no labels; no numbers; no logo; no watermark; no photorealism; no glossy marketing render; no neon glow; no cinematic lighting; no cutaway; no patent-sensitive internal detail; not a tokamak; distinctly stellarator-like twisted external coil geometry; original geometry rather than a copy of the reference; clearly larger and more technical than the tabletop model.
```
