### Required caveat

The five submissions are non-binding expressions of interest. They are not letters of intent, customers, contracts, purchase orders, investment commitments or validated revenue.

### Privacy and permission

- Never place email addresses, submission IDs or full unredacted form screenshots in an external deck.
- Use names and organisations only after confirming permission or a legitimate basis for sharing them with the specific audience.
- If permission is uncertain, identify the signal by role and organisation category, for example `EU energy-policy adviser at a fusion company` or `senior representative from a Danish innovation fund`.

## Public exposure and engagement evidence

### DTU and Novatron fusion lunch lecture

Aurora Confinement organised and promoted the DTU fusion lunch lecture together with Søren Bang Korsholm and Novatron. The team sold out the event within 48 hours.

The available evidence records that:

- 120 people registered within 48 hours.
- A further 20 people joined the waiting list.
- The lecture filled the auditorium and drew students from disciplines across DTU.
- Aurora Confinement led the event organisation and promotion in collaboration with Søren Bang Korsholm and Novatron.

Slide-safe interpretation: Aurora demonstrated an ability to identify an audience, coordinate relevant academic and industry speakers, promote a fusion event and fill the venue quickly. The turnout also demonstrates strong student interest in fusion education and career engagement. It does not demonstrate institutional willingness to pay for Product B.

### Nordic Fusion Forum 2026

Public posts show Aurora team members participating in the Nordic Fusion Forum context and being recognised by external fusion-sector participants. One post describes more than 200 attendees at the forum.

Slide-safe interpretation: this supports team credibility, ecosystem access and the relevance of workforce and public-engagement questions. It does not establish a product market by itself.

## Recommended pitch statements

The following statements are supportable if the underlying screenshots remain available:

- `Since the Open Door pitch, we collected five non-binding expressions of interest across exhibition, research, funding and investment contexts.`
- `Two of the five signals point to concrete near-term exhibition opportunities that Product B could be designed to serve.`
- `Aurora organised and promoted a DTU fusion lunch lecture with Søren Bang Korsholm and Novatron, selling it out within 48 hours with 120 registrations and another 20 people on the waiting list.`
- `The next step is to convert attention into evidence through requirements interviews, prototype demonstrations and conditional pilot discussions.`

Avoid statements such as:

- `Five customers want the product.`
- `Demand is validated.`
- `Innovation Fund Denmark will fund Aurora.`
- `Aurora has secured a European Parliament exhibition.`
- `The event turnout proves willingness to pay.`

## Recommended follow-up evidence

1. Confirm the product configuration and requirements behind the two exhibition-related signals.
2. Run at least 15 structured institutional interviews covering use case, operation, safety, budget, procurement and willingness to buy, rent or sponsor.
3. Secure at least three written letters of interest or documented conditional pilot discussions.
4. Test Product B with at least three demonstrations or structured user sessions when a safe prototype milestone exists.
5. Record owners, dates, outcomes and next actions for all five expressions of interest.
