---
title: 'Introducing Aurora Confinement'
description: 'A student-led initiative working to make stellarator science more tangible and accessible.'
publishedDate: 2026-10-03
---

Aurora Confinement is a student-led initiative formed by 13 students at the Technical University of Denmark. We are developing physical tools that make stellarator technology more tangible and accessible.

Our work currently follows two active directions: tabletop stellarator models for exhibitions, visitor facilities, public engagement, and introductory lectures; and compact experimental platforms for universities, startups, and research teams seeking more accessible hands-on stellarator research.

The initiative is in formation, pre-incorporation, and pre-funding. Both product directions are current priorities, although the experimental platform is at an earlier stage of technical maturity. We are speaking with fusion companies, research institutions, universities, and other organisations to understand where these physical tools could be most useful.

We will use this news section for confirmed project updates. Until details have been reviewed and approved for publication, we will not publish technical performance claims, partnerships, or milestones.
