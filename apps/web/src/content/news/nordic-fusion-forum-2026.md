Members of the Aurora Confinement team took part in Nordic Fusion Forum in Copenhagen on 19 October 2026. It was a chance to introduce the project to researchers, fusion companies, and funders, and to hear what they would want from accessible stellarator tools.

Around the forum we received five non-binding expressions of interest, spanning exhibition, research, funding, and investment contexts. They are early signals. They are not letters of intent, orders, customers, or commitments, and we are following up to understand the requirements behind each one.
