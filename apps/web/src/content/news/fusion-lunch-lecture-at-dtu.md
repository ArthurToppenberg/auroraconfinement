Aurora Confinement organised and promoted a fusion lunch lecture at DTU. The event drew 120 registrations within 48 hours, with a further 20 people on the waiting list, and the auditorium filled with students from disciplines across the university.

The turnout shows how much interest there is in fusion education and in what a career in the field can look like. It is a signal about interest in the subject, not evidence that anyone intends to buy a product from us.

Thank you to everyone who came, and to everyone who helped make the lecture happen.
